# Editorial Policy

**How our news is produced.** TechShield News uses an AI-assisted editorial workflow. For each story, our system reviews reporting from multiple established sources in our coverage areas, then produces an original synthesis that adds context and a practical takeaway for readers. We do not republish or copy articles from other outlets — every piece is written in our own words and links back to the original reporting for attribution.

**Accuracy and review.** Before any article is published, it passes an automated editorial quality check that verifies the piece is on-topic, substantive, and — critically — that every factual claim it makes is supported by the underlying sources. Articles that fail this check are discarded, not published. Our team additionally reviews published output on a regular schedule and corrects or removes anything that falls short.

**Sources and attribution.** We aggregate facts, not text. Articles cite and link to the outlets whose reporting informed them so readers can go to the primary source.

**Corrections.** We take accuracy seriously. If you spot an error, contact us and we will review it promptly. Substantive corrections are noted on the article.

**Independence.** Our coverage decisions are editorial. Any sponsored or affiliate content, if introduced in the future, will be clearly labeled as such and kept separate from news coverage.

# Privacy Policy

This policy explains how TechShield News handles information when you visit this website.

**Information we collect.** We do not require you to create an account or submit personal information to read our articles. Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically log standard technical data such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you visit, for security and aggregate analytics.

**Cookies and analytics.** We may use privacy-respecting analytics to understand which articles are read. If we add advertising in the future, third-party ad providers may use cookies; this policy will be updated to describe those partners and your choices.

**Email.** If you contact us by email, we use your message only to respond to you. We do not sell or share reader email addresses.

**Third-party links.** Our articles link to external sources. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites.

**Your choices.** You can block cookies in your browser settings. For any privacy question, or to request removal of data you believe we hold, contact us at the address on our Contact page.

**Changes.** We may update this policy; material changes will be posted on this page.
